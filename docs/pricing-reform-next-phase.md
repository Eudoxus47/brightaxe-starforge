# Pricing Reform Next Phase

Keep physical material costs and recipes true to the tabletop ledger, then tune the campaign economy with Taark's Perfectionism rather than hidden material-cost shading.

## Intended Model

- Add `perfectionismWastePct` to the forge profile as a DM-adjustable campaign value.
- Treat the waste as rejected work, over-polishing, re-smelting, overbuilt fittings, and other perfectionist losses.
- Calculate recognized commission profit from true revenue, true material costs, normal costs, and perfectionism waste.
- Keep final monthly forecasting centered near the current DM target of about 2,000 gp plus or minus 1,000 gp.
- Let the DM reduce the waste percentage later when Taark levels up, improves processes, trains Jordy, or upgrades the forge.

## Boundary

This is not implemented in the inventory expansion pass. Current profit shading and balance tests remain in place until the pricing model is deliberately replaced.
